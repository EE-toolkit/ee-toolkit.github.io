3. Possible fits · Возможные варианты

The fit score (50 = neutral) compares how much you like the kinds of work each subject mostly involves. The weights are rough estimates and do not predict success. Interest is not ability, and your own teachers know your strengths best. Psychology and global politics are not covered here.

4. Questions to ask before choosing · Вопросы перед выбором

  • Which topics in this subject do I enjoy enough to read about for months? · Какие темы мне интересны надолго?
  • Will I have the data, equipment, sources or participants I need, and by when? · Будут ли данные, оборудование, источники и сроки?
  • Does this subject’s teacher or another supervisor have time to supervise me? · Есть ли свободный супервайзер?
  • What is the first deadline I must meet (First reflection 15–19 Feb 2027) and what do I need by then? · Что нужно к первой дате?
  • If my first idea fails, do I have a second one in the same subject? · Есть ли запасная идея?
