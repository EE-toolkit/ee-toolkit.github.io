4. Key rules · Главные правила APA 7

  • One or two authors: name them every time, (Smith & Lee, 2020) in brackets and Smith and Lee (2020) in a sentence. Three or more: (Smith et al., 2020) from the first citation.
  • Group authors: you may define an abbreviation at the first citation, (World Health Organization [WHO], 2020), then use (WHO, 2020).
  • Direct quotations need a locator: page (p. 12, pp. 12–13), paragraph for web pages (para. 3) or a time stamp for audiovisual works (1:23).
  • No date: (Smith, n.d.). No author: move the title to the author position and cite the title in the text.
  • Titles of articles, chapters and web pages use sentence case. Italicise books, reports, web pages, data sets, films, videos and the names of journals, newspapers and podcasts.
  • Non-English sources: give the title in the original language followed by an English translation in square brackets.
  • Give DOIs as https://doi.org/…; add a retrieval date only for pages designed to change over time.
  • Generative AI (APA Style, September 2025): cite the specific chat as Company. (Year, Month Day). Title of chat [Generative AI chat]. Tool or model. URL of the chat; or cite the tool generally as Company. (Year). Tool or model [Large language model]. URL. In the text: (OpenAI, 2025). Describe how you used AI in your essay, and follow your school’s AI rules.
  • Personal communications (interviews you did, emails) are cited in the text only and do not appear in the reference list.
  • Every in-text citation has a reference-list entry and every entry is cited in the text. In the EE, citations and the reference list do not count towards the 4000 words.
